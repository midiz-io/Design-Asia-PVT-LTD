# TrackFlow Vision - User Manual

Welcome to **TrackFlow Vision**, your comprehensive solution for team management, task tracking, and expense oversight. This manual is designed to help you navigate the system based on your specific role.

---

## 🌟 Application Overview

TrackFlow Vision is a real-time operational platform that connects field teams, managers, and administrators. It streamlines daily operations by:
- **Centralizing Tasks**: Manage and track work progress in real-time.
- **Simplifying Expenses**: Submit and approve petty cash claims with digital receipts.
- **Enhancing Field Safety**: Monitor field team locations and receive safety alerts.
- **Role-Based Insights**: Provide tailored dashboards for every member of your organization.

---

## 👤 Employee Guide

As an Employee, your focus is on executing tasks and managing your day-to-day operational requirements.

### 1. Your Dashboard
Your personal dashboard provides a snapshot of your current work:
- **Task Overview**: See how many tasks are assigned, in progress, or completed.
- **Recent Activity**: Quickly view your latest task updates and expense submissions.
- **Quick Actions**: Buttons to quickly submit a new expense or update your status.

### 2. Managing Tasks
- **View Tasks**: Access your assigned tasks from the sidebar.
- **Update Status**: Change task status (e.g., "In Progress" to "Completed") as you work.
- **Attachments**: You can upload photos or documents as proof of work directly to a task.

### 3. Expense Claims (Petty Cash)
- **Submit a Claim**: Go to the "Petty Cash" section and click "New Request".
- **Upload Receipts**: **Crucial!** Always take a photo of your receipt and upload it with your request.
- **Track Approval**: Monitor whether your request is "Pending", "Approved", or "Rejected" in real-time.

---

## 👥 Manager Guide

Managers oversee team performance and handle the primary approval workflows.

### 1. Team Dashboard
- **Team Stats**: View completion rates and active task counts for your entire team.
- **Analytics**: Identify bottlenecks or top performers through visual charts.

### 2. Approvals
- **Review Expenses**: You will receive notifications for expense claims submitted by your team.
- **Decision Making**: Review the attached receipt, check against the budget, and "Approve" or "Reject" with comments.
- **Budget Tracking**: Use the "Budget Tracker" to ensure your team stays within allocated limits.

### 3. Task Assignment
- **Create Tasks**: Assign specific jobs to team members with deadlines and detailed instructions.

---

## 🛰️ Supervisor Guide

Supervisors focus on field operations, real-time tracking, and safety.

### 1. Field Monitoring
- **Live Location**: View the real-time location of field staff on the map.
- **Status Alerts**: Receive alerts if a team member's phone battery is low or if they have been inactive for an extended period.

### 2. Geofencing
- **Safety Zones**: Monitor when team members enter or exit predefined "Geofences" (work sites).
- **Automated Alerts**: Get notified automatically when a site visit begins or ends.

---

## 🛡️ Admin Guide

Administrators manage the system configuration and user access.

### 1. User Management
- **Role Assignment**: Assign or change user roles (Employee, Supervisor, Manager, Admin).
- **Account Control**: Manage user access and profiles.

### 2. System Settings
- **Category Management**: Define expense categories and budget limits.
- **System Metrics**: Overview of system-wide activity and health.

## 🔄 Common Workflows

### How to Submit a Receipt for Reimbursement
1. Navigate to **Petty Cash** from the sidebar.
2. Click the **+ New Expense** button.
3. Enter the **Amount**, select a **Category**, and provide a brief **Description**.
4. Click the **Upload** area to attach a photo or PDF of your receipt.
5. Click **Submit**. You will be notified once a Manager reviews it.

### How to Complete a Task
1. Open the **Tasks** page.
2. Click on the task you are working on.
3. Update the status to **In Progress** while working.
4. Once finished, upload any required files and change the status to **Completed**.

## 💬 Communication & Notifications

### Notifications
- Look for the **Bell Icon** in the top right corner.
- Red badges indicate unread updates (new tasks, approval decisions, etc.).
- Click a notification to go directly to the relevant page.

### Real-Time Chat
- Use the **Chat** feature to communicate directly with team members or managers about specific tasks or field issues.

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: I submitted an expense but haven't heard back.**
*A: Check the "Pending" tab in Petty Cash. You can see which manager the request is currently with.*

**Q: My location is not updating.**
*A: Ensure you have granted the application "Always Allow" location permissions in your mobile browser settings.*

**Q: How do I change my password?**
*A: Go to your **Profile** settings via the user icon in the top right.*
